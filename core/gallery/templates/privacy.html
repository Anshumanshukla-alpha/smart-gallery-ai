Privacy notice

Smart Gallery AI processes photos that you upload to organize your personal gallery. Face detection and grouping are performed to provide the app's people-search features.

Upload only photos you have the right to use and, where needed, permission to process. Do not upload sensitive images or photos of others without their consent.

Uploaded images and derived face crops are associated with your account. You can delete individual photos in the app or delete your account to remove your account records. Production deployments should use private object storage with retention and deletion controls.

This demo is not designed for biometric identification, surveillance, or decisions about people.
